McDonald's isn't a burger company. Behind every counter, there is something else. Over sixty percent of its revenue comes from franchisees. The annual report says it plainly: franchisees pay rent and royalties. And rent keeps growing: nine point eight billion dollars in twenty twenty-three. From Chicago to Tokyo, from London to Sydney, the restaurants sit on prime land. They own the land under more than half of them. McDonald's makes billions from real estate. Behind the burgers, it's a landlord.
